# M2.1 Visual refinement — 2026-11-02

Base: `feat/m1-frontend-foundation` at `06799ae` plus the uncommitted M2 Journey work
(`docs/milestones/m2-journey-frontend.md`). **Not committed.** Frontend only: no backend file,
API contract, CSP or safety policy changed. The parallel `feat/v0.10-evidence-intelligence`
worktree was not touched. The isotipo in `frontend/public/branding/` is unchanged.

Review gallery (real screenshots, before/after): https://claude.ai/artifact/F9i818bsKu7qjbzbS9wjb2

The goal was restraint in the Projects hero, more character and depth in the secondary
mastheads, correct scale and contrast for the ASCII characters, a substantial Compare upgrade
and selective refinement of the technical workspaces.

## Main hero (Projects)

`art/JourneyScene.tsx`, recomposed. Hierarchy, strongest first: rider, copy and actions,
settlement, moon and halo, binary texture.

- **Settlement**: the same original architectural primitives, at 0.7× (was 1.32×), raised
  onto the far ridge with its base hidden by the hill. It reads by atmospheric perspective: a
  pale violet-grey horizon haze behind it, a low-contrast dark fill, a softer rim, four lit
  windows instead of seven. Finials end in a small lozenge; the cross bars are gone (gothic
  without explicit religious iconography).
- **Moon**: radius 23 (was 38), desaturated silver-lavender with violet only at the limb,
  0.86 opacity, finer dither, glow and halo rings at roughly half their previous strength. It
  sits on the skyline with the crossing spire passing in front of its lower edge.
- **Rider**: now drawn inside the scene SVG, as a tonal fill (bone-white head and shoulders,
  violet only at the hooves) masked by the unmodified `muerte.cutout.png`
  (`mask-type: alpha`). It stands on its own ridge at every width because it shares the scene's
  coordinates. A soft shadow pool behind it separates it from sky, halo and field; fog is
  layered behind it, never over it; the bottom page fade shrank from 64 to 28 px so the hooves
  stay on the ridge. The HTML `.journey-rider` element remains only as the `data-character`
  marker.
- **Stars**: the one beside the rider's face (and the two in the rider's area) are removed;
  five small marks remain over the settlement and open sky.
- Background violet wash reduced (0.22 → 0.13) and moved to the moon; a dark wash sits behind
  the rider. Binary field: lower density, light centred on the moon.

## Secondary masthead family

`art/MastArt.tsx` adds `MastScene` (the `art` of a `PageMast`) and five original engraved
ornaments built from two-centred pointed arches and rings. Each scene is masked so it is
strongest at the right edge and gone before the copy column (`transparent 0 34% → #000 70%`).

| Kind | Used on | Composition |
|---|---|---|
| Figure | Tools, Rules (bundled), Rules (stored), Import | One character on a shadow pool and violet backlight, an ornament behind at reduced weight |
| Architecture | Project workspace, Version, Compare, Report preview | `Ruins` (broken tower, fallen arch on a horizon), `Arcade` (three bays, the last broken), `TwinLights` (a two-light window) with a faint binary field |
| Instrument | Run | `Astrolabe` edge (rings, tick ring, alidade), no field: a quiet strip for a dense workstation |

Ornaments step left of the mast's actions (`.mast:has(.mast-side)`), so buttons never sit on
artwork. On phones a figure gets its own 150 px strip under the copy instead of standing
behind it; architecture fades in from the bottom.

## Visibility fixes

The masks are glyph-density fields: small renders lose density, so one opacity cannot serve all
eight. Per placement:
- New `bone` tint (pale at head and shoulders, violet only at the feet).
- `.mast-figure` / `.art-dense` print the masked tone twice (a `::before` with
  `background: inherit` under the same mask; alpha `1 − (1 − a)²`). The mystic, the lightest
  engraving, gets a third impression and a brighter tone.
- Per-figure `--fig-right / --fig-top / --fig-h` so every head starts inside its frame.
- **Crow**: moon removed (`DitherMoon` is no longer used), figure at 1.0 opacity with its top
  22 px inside the frame, a shadow pool behind it; phones give it a 240 px strip under the form.
- **Automaton**: was a faint ash crop with the head lost; now framed whole above the mast
  bottom, in front of the astrolabe.
- **Wizard**: was a dark oversized crop; now whole inside a pointed niche.
- **Scavenger**: concept kept; its head was clipped (top at −10 %), now at +5 %, bone tint.
- **Nohead**: 0.45 → 0.62 opacity; below 1024 px it stands under the 404 copy instead of
  behind it (this also fixed an axe contrast failure on the lede at those widths).

## Components

- `Notice` (components.tsx): a small rounded badge with a warning or info icon and a label,
  on a neutral surface with a toned left edge; the text stays normal readable text, role
  `note`. Used on Compare ("Limitation", "Identical bytes", "Not comparable").
- `.notice-warn` (all other warnings: Version warnings, Run banner): the amber fill is replaced
  by a neutral raised surface, an amber edge and a faint warm wash at the edge only.
- `.plate`: an engraved instrument plate (subtle top light, corner brackets, dashed foot) for
  tool cards and the rule-set identity card. Available tools carry a thin violet edge light;
  unavailable ones are dashed and unlit. Availability text and versions are unchanged.
- Rules codex: rule entries numbered with an engraved lozenge, a double hairline between
  entries, a plum gutter on the source. Text is untouched and literal.
- Band labels (Understand / Investigate / Record) gain an engraved lozenge where the double
  rule starts. Tables, code, findings and byte maps carry no texture.

## Compare

- Atmospheric mast with the two-light window.
- **Bench**: A and B side by side around an axis with a lozenge, each with a serif letter
  mark, the version selector, "Before"/"After", the short SHA-256 and the analysis status for
  that side, and its "View version" link. Stacks on phones.
- **Category strip**: a tab row (`aria-pressed` kept) whose tabs show the real counts
  (`+added −removed ~changed`) or the category's status (e.g. *unavailable*). Counts are
  `aria-hidden`; the full sentences remain as an `sr-only` "Comparison summary" group, and the
  run status line is kept for assistive technology.
- **Change ledger**: each change is a card with a toned edge (added violet, changed lilac,
  removed neutral grey: no red/green verdict colours), the key in mono and Before (A) / After (B)
  as two columns of one table-like frame.
- Export actions in a framed row; pagination aligned.
No comparison data is invented; nothing claims equivalence beyond what the API reports.

## Known limitations

- The settlement and ornaments are composed from SVG primitives, not hand-engraved plates. At
  close range they read as clean vector line work beside the ASCII figures. A commissioned
  engraved city and ruin set (VISUAL_ASSET_GUIDE) would close the remaining gap to the boards.
- The masks are used unmodified; the double impression raises density uniformly, so very
  sparse regions of an engraving stay sparse.
- `art/DitherMoon.tsx` is no longer used (kept, not deleted).
- Run's Understand / Investigate panels received only the band ornament; their internals are
  unchanged from M2.
- Owner review of the rendered screens is still required.
